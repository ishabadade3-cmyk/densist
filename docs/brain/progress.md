## Assumptions
- Clinical content in `apps/web/config/clinic-content.ts` uses objective, educational medical phrasing without promotional or outcome-guaranteeing claims, complying with Dental Council of India and ASCI norms.
- All clinic-specific facts (name, phone, address, prices, credentials) remain registered as TBD keys pending clinic owner approval.
- An environment variable `ALLOW_UNVERIFIED_CONTENT=1` is provided to allow operators to build preview and CI environments while production builds without this flag fail until TBDs are verified.
- T-008 walk-in accounts are created with `pending` status and are not activated by receptionist registration; clinic activation and contact-verification policy remains TBD.
- T-008 uses the explicit transition proposal `requested -> confirmed|cancelled`, `confirmed -> cancelled|completed|no-show`, with cancelled/completed/no-show terminal. Clinic approval of the transition graph and cancellation/reschedule policy remains pending in OQ-004; rescheduling is rejected until policy is configured.
- T-008 bounds schedule-calendar queries to 93 days and aggregate-report queries to 366 days pending approval of operational query ranges (OQ-037).
- Admin reports currently expose aggregate appointment counts grouped by day or status only; further report metrics remain outside this implementation until defined.
- Website CMS records start as drafts; publication approval and content retention are unresolved in OQ-035/OQ-036.
- T-011 invoice references and receipt numbers use random UUID-derived values; invoice composition remains amount-only with tax and line-item rules unresolved. The reconciliation CLI is intended for an external scheduler, currently with no schedule configured and a 600-second default stale-payment threshold. Offline refund execution remains external pending policy. Legacy `verified` payment statuses are preserved pending an approved reconciliation plan.
- T-012 records channel-specific consent, and legacy channel-less consent never authorizes a notification. Clinical care, appointment reminders, payment updates, and marketing use separate purpose values; marketing templates cannot use dynamic variables. Provider adapters are deployment-loaded through `NOTIFICATION_PROVIDER_FACTORY`; no vendor was selected or credentials invented. WhatsApp remains an interface stub until v1.1. SMS requires a manually marked approved DLT template ID and sender ID; URLs are blocked pending a confirmed whitelist. The worker defaults to three attempts with exponential backoff from 60 seconds; reminder scheduling runs only with explicit lead-time/template configuration and static approved reminder templates. Channel cascade, copy, DLT approval, provider terms and retention remain in OQ-050–OQ-055.
- T-013 has no approved clinical urgency rules. The development placeholder broadly escalates symptom/medicine/safety-sensitive text, provides no clinic-specific instructions, is opt-in outside production, and production assistant endpoints stay disabled. Provider model and embedding adapter, tokenizer, dimensions, character chunk approximation (2,400 characters with 300 overlap), exact-search index strategy and retrieval distance (0.35), per-minute/daily quotas (5/50), seven-day pseudonymous usage-counter retention and cleanup cadence remain explicit assumptions/TBDs in OQ-056–OQ-060. Cost estimates use only `services.price_min/price_max` and units; no LLM is called.
