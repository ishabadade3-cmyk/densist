# Clinic staff guide

Use only your individually assigned account and role. Do not share passwords, MFA codes, sessions, screenshots or patient information. The deployed URL and role-specific onboarding contact will be supplied by the clinic after approval.

## Receptionist

- Use the receptionist workspace for calendar queries, appointment status actions and walk-in registration within your permissions.
- Confirm patient identity using the clinic's approved procedure before changing a record. That procedure is TBD and must be approved by the clinic.
- The receptionist interface is designed not to show clinical notes. Do not seek clinical content through another account, export, URL or tool. Route clinical questions to authorized clinical staff.
- Collect only details required by the booking or registration workflow, and explain consent choices through approved clinic wording.

## Dentist

- Use your assigned dentist account and access only patients within your care assignment.
- Review history and allergy alerts before care. Enter clinical notes, treatment stages, prescriptions and reports only through authorized workflows.
- Check document release controls before making clinical documents visible to patients. Record only clinically relevant information and follow the approved clinical retention and correction policy.
- For urgent symptoms, follow the clinic's clinician-approved escalation protocol. Do not rely on an unapproved assistant urgency response.

## All staff

- Use approved notification channels and templates. Marketing communications require separate recorded consent.
- Never record patient identifiers, symptoms, passwords, OTPs, payment data or clinical details in console output, browser diagnostics, chat, screenshots, or general incident tickets.
- Sign out when finished. Report access errors, misdirected notifications, suspicious activity, lost devices, or suspected data exposure immediately using `incident-response.md`.
- Do not promise clinical outcomes or use patient images/testimonials without the approved consent and publication process.

Clinic contacts, identity-check steps, appointment handling policy, notification wording, clinical escalation protocol, document release policy and retention schedule are TBD and must be supplied by authorized owners before live training.
